Configure PUBLIC_BASE_URL when sharing public links. Enable TRUST_PROXY=1 only behind the included Caddy proxy, with direct app access restricted.

Data & maintenance

SQLite holds links, visits, and events. Media lives outside the public web directory and requires administrator authentication.

Links, visits, events, and uploaded media are kept until you explicitly delete them. Link expiration stops new visits; it never deletes the link or its records. Automatic cleanup is disabled, including the legacy cleanup command and RETENTION_DAYS setting.

Back up ./data, ./tls, and ./caddy-config on the host. Treat backups and exports as sensitive: they contain IP addresses, location, and submitted media.

IP addresses may identify VPNs, proxies, or shared networks. Device details and coordinates are browser-reported, and requests may be automated. No third-party analytics or IP geolocation service is used.
